Checks & notes

The assembled base and all 34 translation stages were checked. The viewer uses the exact checked path. Rotation is paused and returns to zero during explosion. Threads are assumed released before withdrawal; designed flexible tooth preload is intentional.

Four 625ZZ bearings tilt 45° on integral printed axles, with one rounded block each and no roller hardware. They run on the ring's continuous 45° outer bevel. The ring is 176 mm OD; flat-seated button-head screws remain inside the track and clear of the rollers. Reprint the plate, ring and Ø15.25 spindle when updating from the flat-base kit. The spindle has a 0.5 mm top chamfer. Left drag orbits, middle drag pans, and the wheel zooms; right-click opens download, Isolate, Hide part and Show All options. Show All works on empty space and restores hardware and clearance envelopes.

The base now has five R6-cornered cutouts, reducing solid CAD volume by about 37%. A 10 mm rim, diagonal webs and mounting pads remain. The rotating deck has five R3-cornered windows for 25.2% less solid CAD volume, with its full Z-cradle footprints, bearing hub, ribs and outer rim preserved. The bearings and integral blocks now move 0.4 mm toward the track along its normal (0.283 mm inward and upward), creating intentional nominal overlap. The central Ø15.25 mm spindle for the 6002 bearings has four 0.5 mm-wide relief slots, 33.1 mm long, with rounded ends and a 0.5 mm connected root. The small roller axles are solid again. Reprint part 05; also replace part 01 if you printed the slotted-roller v4.8 base. A v4.7 solid-axle base remains compatible. Actual preload and free rotation are untested. The remaining underside stays flat. Printed press fits are checked separately from rigid collisions; actual fit and load capacity remain untested.
